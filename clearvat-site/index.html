What we do

One visit. Three jobs. One number on the fryer.

You keep buying your own oil from your own supplier. We make every jug go further and give you a reading you can show the health inspector.

01

Filter

We pump hot oil through 5 micron filter paper with a portable filter cart. Crumbs, carbon and batter are what wear oil out fastest, so we take them out.

  • Every fryer, every visit
  • Done while oil is still hot
  • Crumbs bagged and taken away
02

Test

We take a TPM reading on every vat with a professional Testo 270 and log it with a photo. You get a running history, so a change decision is a number, not a guess.

  • Reading texted after each visit
  • Monthly log for inspections
  • Change only when it reads 24%
03

Clean

We scrub the vat and elements, wipe the outside and top up the oil. On change day we drain, deep clean, refill and move the used oil to your collector bin.

  • Carbon crust off before it builds
  • Food-safe degreaser only
  • Used oil to your collector bin
How we do it

A visit, minute by minute.

We do it during the quiet hour after close, while the oil is still warm and your closer is still on site. A two-fryer kitchen takes about 45 minutes.

10:40

Arrive at close

Your closer lets us in. Fryers are switched off but the oil is still hot, which is when it filters best.

10:45

Read every vat

We take a Testo 270 TPM reading and a photo of each fryer before we touch anything.

10:50

Filter

The cart pumps oil out through 5 µm paper. The vat sits empty for cleaning.

11:05

Scrub

We scrub walls, elements and baskets, then wipe down the outside. The filtered oil goes back in and gets topped up.

11:25

Log & text

You get the reading, a photo and a plain call: keep frying, or change on a set day.

Questions

Things owners ask us.

Does someone need to stay late for you?

Only your closer, who is usually on site anyway. Most kitchens give us a key fob or a lockbox code after the first visit, so nobody has to wait around.

Is it safe to handle oil that hot?

Yes. The filter cart is built to pump hot oil and we wear heat-rated gloves and aprons. We lay spill mats before we start and never carry open containers of hot oil across your floor.

Do I have to buy oil from you?

No. You keep your own supplier and price. We only top up or refill with the oil you already have on the shelf.

What happens to the used oil?

On change day we move it into your existing collector bin for your rendering company. If you don't have a collector yet, we'll help you set one up.

Which areas do you cover?

Surrey City Centre, Whalley, Guildford, Newton, Fleetwood and Cloverdale. If you're in North Delta or Langley, ask us. We often fit you in.

What's the catch with the free two weeks?

There isn't one. We do two full Filter & Care visits and show you the readings. If you like what you see, we agree a schedule. If not, you owe nothing and we leave on good terms.
