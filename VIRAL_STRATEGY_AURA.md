# AURA — Viral Growth Strategy

> **One-liner:** An AI that reads your selfie + your friends' anonymous one-word
> answers and renders your "aura" as a shareable, glowing card. You can't see who
> voted unless you pay. Built to parasite iMessage, Snap, and IG Stories.

The category is proven (NGL, Gas, Tellonym, Slay). Those products died because
they were *static text*. AURA wins because the payload is a **kinetic, glowing,
screenshot-bait visual** that begs to be reposted, and the curiosity loop is
generated by an LLM instead of a fixed question bank — so it never goes stale.

---

## 1. The Core Dopamine Hook

You open the app, snap a selfie, and in **under two seconds** an AI paints a
living "aura" around you — a swirling gradient with a one-word verdict
(`MAGNETIC`, `UNHINGED`, `SOFT`, `MENACE`). It feels like a Snapchat lens crossed
with a horoscope. That card is *yours*, it's beautiful, and the only way to find
out what your **friends** think your aura is... is to drop your link and let them
vote anonymously. Every vote is a fresh hit. The card visually *evolves* as votes
come in — the gradient shifts, the haptics pulse. Watching your own aura mutate in
real time is the slot-machine pull.

## 2. Onboarding Flow (< 5 seconds, zero friction)

1. **Tap "See My Aura."** No signup, no email, no password. Anonymous device
   identity minted silently (Supabase anonymous auth).
2. **Front camera fires instantly.** One tap → selfie captured.
3. **Aura renders.** AI returns the gradient + word with a haptic *thunk*. User is
   already looking at their core utility. **This is the whole first session.**
4. Consent + push-permission prompts come *after* the dopamine, framed as
   "Turn on alerts so you know the second someone reads your aura."

No surveys. No "tell us about yourself." The product *shows* before it *asks*.

## 3. The Host-Platform Parasitic Loop

AURA never builds a social graph. It feeds on networks the user already has.

- **The Share Sticker:** After the first aura renders, the only forward action is
  **"Get my friends' read →"**. This generates a personalized link
  (`aura.app/u/<handle>`) pre-loaded into a **native IG Story / Snap link sticker**
  with the glowing card as the background. One tap, it's on their Story.
- **The Vote = Forced Install:** A friend taps the sticker, lands on a web
  micro-app, and to cast their one-word anonymous vote they tap "Use the app to
  reply" → App Store. Voting *is* onboarding for the next user. They land, snap
  their own selfie, and the loop forks.
- **iMessage tendril:** Native share-sheet drop into group chats — the link
  unfurls as a rich card showing the live aura, not a dead URL.

**Viral coefficient target:** `K = i × c > 1`

- `i` (shares/invites per user): drive ≥ 3 by gating the *fun part* (friends'
  votes) behind sharing — you literally cannot get votes without distributing the
  link.
- `c` (share → activated user conversion): target ≥ 0.4 by making the vote action
  itself require the app, so every curious friend becomes an install, not a
  bounce.

At `i = 3, c = 0.4`, `K = 1.2` → self-sustaining exponential pull. Instrument both
variables per-cohort and treat any drop below `K = 1` as a sev-1.

## 4. The Monetization Trigger (the cliffhanger)

The paywall hits at the **moment of maximum curiosity**, never before.

- Votes roll in anonymously. The card glows brighter with each one. A push fires:
  **"3 people just rated your aura. One of them said 'OBSESSED' 👀 — see who."**
- User opens the app, sees the votes, taps a vote to reveal the sender → **hard
  paywall.** The blurred name sits *right there*.
- **Offer: AURA+ at $4.99/week** (curiosity tax, weekly micro-sub — recurs while
  the social loop is hot, not a cold monthly plan). Unlocks: who voted, hint
  drops ("they're in your contacts," "first initial: J"), and rarer AI aura styles.
- **The Curiosity Notification Loop** keeps re-triggering the paywall: every new
  vote = new open-loop push = new reveal attempt = another collision with the
  blurred name. The cliffhanger renews itself for free.

## 5. The BFF Tech Stack (zero client-side key leaks)

**Non-negotiable:** no OpenAI / Anthropic / Supabase service keys ever compiled
into the app binary or sitting in a client-side `.env`. Every model call is
proxied.

```
[ Expo / React Native client ]
        │  (anon JWT only — no provider keys on device)
        ▼
[ BFF: serverless functions — Firebase Cloud Functions / AWS Lambda ]
        │  ├── holds Anthropic + image keys in secret manager
        │  ├── rate-limits per device, validates payloads
        │  └── runs the aura-generation prompt (claude-opus-4-8 for the
        │       verdict, image model for the gradient render)
        ▼
[ Supabase Postgres ]
        ├── Row-Level Security ON for every table (no wildcard policies)
        ├── votes table: voter identity readable ONLY by the BFF, never the client
        └── reveal gated server-side behind verified AURA+ entitlement
```

Hard rules:
- **BFF proxy** for all AI traffic. Keys live in a secret manager, never in the
  bundle.
- **RLS enforced** on every table; the anonymous voter's identity is *physically
  unreadable* from the client — the reveal is computed server-side only after the
  subscription receipt is verified. (Also kills the trivial "inspect the network
  tab to unmask voters" exploit that has nuked every prior app in this category.)
- **Dependency guardrail:** every package vetted by hand before install — no blind
  `npm install` on model-suggested names. Slopsquatting/typosquatting is a
  supply-chain landmine.

### Why this compounds where the others flatlined

NGL and Gas monetized *text*. Text doesn't get reposted — it gets screenshotted
once and forgotten. AURA monetizes a **living visual** that is (a) gorgeous enough
to live on a Story for 24h of free distribution, (b) regenerated by an LLM so the
content well never runs dry, and (c) wrapped in a curiosity loop where the payoff
literally cannot be reached without recruiting the next cohort. The share *is* the
product. The product *is* the ad.
